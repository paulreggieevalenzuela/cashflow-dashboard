## 1. Summary

Once the Cashflow system goes live in January 2027, it has to stay online, safe and working every day. This plan covers that for a fixed monthly fee of **PHP 2,500 to PHP 4,000**, depending on how much support the clinic wants. The fee includes the hosting, the database and the web address (domain) the system runs on, so the clinic does not have to open or pay for any of those accounts.

| | **Essentials** | **Standard** |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly fee** | **PHP 2,500** | **PHP 4,000** |
| Hosting, database and domain kept running | Included | Included |
| Uptime monitoring and security updates | Included | Included |
| Monthly backup copy of the data, with a restore check | Included | Included |
| Fixing faults (something stops working or a figure is wrong) | Included | Included |
| Support and small changes | Up to **1 hour** a month | Up to **2 hours** a month |
| Response to a fault during clinic days | Next business day | Same business day |
| Extra work beyond the included hours | PHP 1,000 per hour | PHP 1,000 per hour |

*Recommended: **Standard** for the first six months, while staff are still getting used to the system and small adjustments are most likely.*

## 2. What the monthly fee pays for

### a) The services the system runs on

The system needs three paid services. They are charged in US dollars to a card, so the peso amount moves slightly with the exchange rate.

| Service | What it is for | Approx. cost per month |
|---|---|---|
| **Hosting** (Vercel, Pro plan) | Runs the website and keeps it online. The free plan is for personal, non-commercial use only, so a clinic's system needs the paid plan. | US$20 (about PHP 1,160) |
| **Database** (Neon, Launch plan) | Stores every patient record, payment, expense and uploaded receipt, with backups. Charged by actual use, so it stays small for one clinic. | About US$5–12 (about PHP 290–700) |
| **Domain name** | The web address staff type in (for example, adt-cashflow.com). | About US$1–2 (about PHP 60–120) |
| **Total** | | **About US$26–34 (about PHP 1,500–2,000)** |

Peso figures use roughly PHP 58 per US$1 and the vendors' published prices as of October 2026.

### b) The care and attention

Whatever is left after the services above pays for the developer's time: watching the system, applying security updates, taking and checking backups, answering questions and fixing problems.

| | Essentials (PHP 2,500) | Standard (PHP 4,000) |
|---|---|---|
| Services above | about PHP 1,500–2,000 | about PHP 1,500–2,000 |
| Developer time | about PHP 500–1,000 (up to 1 hour) | about PHP 2,000–2,500 (up to 2 hours) |

## 3. Why this price

1. **It is close to cost.** Most of the Essentials fee (about 60–80%) is the real, unavoidable cost of keeping the system online. The clinic pays for the services at the price the vendors charge, not at a mark-up.
2. **The paid plans are required.** The free hosting plan's terms do not allow business use. The free database plan has only 1 GB of storage (invoice photos and receipts would fill it within weeks) and keeps only 6 hours of history. A clinic's financial records need real backups.
3. **The cost grows only with use.** The database is billed by use. Even with daily receipt photos, storage grows by about 5 GB a year, which adds roughly PHP 100 a month for each year of use. The fee stays flat until that changes the picture.
4. **The data is sensitive.** The system holds patient names and the clinic's finances. Security updates, backups and a tested restore are not optional extras; they are what protects the clinic if something goes wrong.
5. **It is far below what agencies charge.** Local agencies commonly quote PHP 20,000 a month or more to maintain a business website, and PHP 50,000 and up for sites that hold financial or personal data. This plan is deliberately lean: it keeps the system running and fixed, and anything bigger is quoted separately.
6. **It keeps costs predictable.** One fixed monthly amount, with no surprise dollar charges, and any extra work is agreed before it is done.

## 4. What is not included

- **New features and enhancements** (for example, payment reminders, inventory, forecasting or other items on the Future Enhancements list). These are quoted separately.
- **Training sessions** for new staff, and re-importing or correcting large amounts of old data.
- **The project materials** discussed in the project proposal.
- **Problems caused outside the system**, such as the clinic's internet connection, devices or a vendor's outage (the developer will still help diagnose them).

Work that is not included is never started without the clinic's agreement. It is either charged at **PHP 1,000 per hour** or quoted as a fixed price when it is larger. Unused included hours do not carry over to the next month.

---

## 5. Terms

- **Start date:** from deployment (January 2027). The first 30 days after launch are covered by the project price: defects in what was built are fixed at no charge.
- **Minimum term:** 6 months, then month to month. Either side can end the plan with 30 days' notice.
- **Billing:** monthly, in advance, in pesos (bank transfer or GCash).
- **The clinic owns its data.** At any time the clinic can ask for a full copy of its data, and on request or at the end of the plan the hosting accounts are handed over to the clinic with a reasonable handover period.
- **Price review:** the fee is reviewed once a year, or earlier if the vendors' prices or the exchange rate change the cost of the services by more than 20%. Any change is given in writing at least 30 days ahead.
- **Faults are fixed first.** If the system is down or showing wrong money figures, it is treated as urgent and does not wait for the included hours to be used.
